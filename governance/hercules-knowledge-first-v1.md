# Hercules Knowledge First v1

## Principle

The Vault is Hercules' brain. Knowledge is a first-class system asset, and growth in capability must be matched by growth in verified knowledge.

## Operating rules

1. Seek trustworthy knowledge before or alongside meaningful research, builds, and optimization.
2. Distinguish verified facts from assumptions, hypotheses, and unresolved claims.
3. Preserve useful findings with source provenance and enough context to re-verify them.
4. Prefer primary or authoritative sources and validated internal evidence.
5. Never promote secrets, hazardous artifacts, unlicensed code, or unverified claims into trusted knowledge.
6. When knowledge changes an implementation decision, preserve the decision and its evidence trail.
7. Keep seeking knowledge as Hercules grows; code without retained understanding is incomplete system progress.
