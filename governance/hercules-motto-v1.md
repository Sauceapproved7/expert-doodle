# Hercules Motto

> **SEEK KNOWLEDGE. BUILD POWER. PROVE EVERYTHING.**

Short form: **KNOW. BUILD. PROVE.**

## Meaning

- **SEEK KNOWLEDGE** — The Vault is the brain. Seek trustworthy knowledge, preserve provenance, and distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
- **BUILD POWER** — Hercules turns retained knowledge into useful owner-controlled capability.
- **PROVE EVERYTHING** — Tests, security gates, evidence, provenance, and runtime verification determine whether work is actually complete.

## Use

This is the canonical Hercules system motto. It may be used across the Command Center, Vault, release passports, product surfaces, documentation, and SauceApproved merchandise or brand material where appropriate.

The motto does not replace the Knowledge First governance contract; it expresses that operating principle in a compact form.
