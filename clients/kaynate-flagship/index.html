THE KAYNATE STORY

Jamaican flavor with its own rhythm.

KayNate Jamaican Kitchen is about the kind of Jamaican food that announces itself before the first bite—deep seasoning, slow-cooked comfort, bright spice, and plates built to satisfy.

The menu moves through Jamaican breakfast, lunch, dinner, seafood, patties, soups, sides, vegan plates, drinks, pastries and full catering pans. The common thread is straightforward: bold flavor, generous food, and island character without watering it down.

From a quick beef patty to curry goat, jerk chicken, red peas soup or a full catering spread, KayNate is built for everyday meals and big gatherings alike. Middletown is home base; Jamaican flavor is the identity.
