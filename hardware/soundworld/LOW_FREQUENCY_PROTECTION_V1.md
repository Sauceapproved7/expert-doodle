# SoundWorld V1 — Low-Frequency Protection & Limiter Model

The EVT-A protection layer treats loudness and hardware survival as one problem.

## Screening policy
- Driver and passive-radiator predicted excursion are independently checked.
- EVT screening reserves 15% of stated linear excursion as margin.
- Below passive-radiator tuning, the provisional voltage ceiling tapers aggressively because driver unloading can increase excursion.
- Either driver or radiator over-travel blocks the candidate.
- Scene Mode and Hercules Link may request tonal/output changes but cannot raise immutable protection ceilings.

## Evidence required to replace provisional curves
Measured/nonlinear driver and PR behavior; enclosure impedance sweep; Klippel-equivalent large-signal data when available; amplifier rail/limiter calibration; microphone response; thermal compression; physical excursion correlation.

## EVT prototype acceptance
A prototype is not approved merely because it sounds loud. It must remain inside excursion and thermal limits across the defined sweep/program material and recover predictably from limiter engagement.

No max-SPL, bass-extension or distortion claim is authorized by this screening model.
