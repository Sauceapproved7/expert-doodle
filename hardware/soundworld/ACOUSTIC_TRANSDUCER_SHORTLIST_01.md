# SoundWorld V1 — Acoustic Transducer Shortlist 01

Status: manufacturer-data desk screen only. No purchasing or production freeze authorized.

## Mid-bass / active driver
PRIMARY STUDY: Dayton Audio ND91-4, 3.5-inch, 4 ohm.
Manufacturer-published inputs: Fs 74 Hz, Qts 0.41, Vas 1.4 L, Sd 30.4 cm2, Xmax 4.6 mm, Vd 12.2 cm3, 30 W RMS, 85.6 dB @ 2.83 V/1 m. Manufacturer also publishes Klippel results/data files and 3D scans. Stronger excursion candidate than DMA80-4 (2.5 mm Xmax / 7.8 cm3 Vd). Must be remeasured from physical samples before freeze.

ALTERNATE STUDY: Dayton Audio ND91-8. Published Xmax 5.1 mm and Vd 12.2 cm3, but lower published sensitivity at 2.83 V. Electrical match must be assessed with the chosen amplifier/rail.

## Tweeter
PRIMARY STUDY: Dayton Audio ND20FA-6, 0.75-inch soft dome, 6 ohm.
Published: Fs 2005 Hz, 90 dB @ 1 W/1 m, 15 W RMS, usable 3.5–25 kHz, 45 mm OD / 15 mm depth. Candidate crossover study must remain above its safe measured operating region; no final crossover is frozen.

## Passive radiator
PRIMARY STUDY: Dayton Audio ND90-PR, 3.5-inch.
Published: Sd 31.2 cm2, Xmax 9 mm, Fs 53.8 Hz, adjustable mass. Its high excursion is attractive, but the final system must satisfy the Hercules >=2x total active-driver displacement screen using measured rather than catalog values.

SECONDARY STUDY: Dayton Audio DMA80-PR, 3-inch.
Published: Sd 31.2 cm2, Xmax 7 mm, Vd 21.8 cm3, Fs 34.6 Hz, adjustable mass. Compact packaging candidate.

## Critical displacement gate
Two active ND91-4 catalog Vd values total about 24.4 cm3. Two DMA80-PR catalog Vd values total about 43.6 cm3, below a strict 2x active-displacement target of about 48.8 cm3. Therefore that pairing is HOLD under the current conservative Hercules rule unless measured values or architecture change.

ND90-PR catalog data gives 31.2 cm2 * 9 mm = about 28.1 cm3 per radiator by geometric screening, about 56.2 cm3 for two. That clears the desk-level 2x screen against 24.4 cm3 active Vd, but still requires physical nonlinear/excursion validation.

## Required sample evidence before freeze
Measure T/S on multiple active-driver samples; impedance and distortion sweeps; compression vs power; excursion; passive-radiator added-mass tuning and nonlinear travel; tweeter distortion/directivity; tolerance spread; thermal endurance; gasket/leak effects; installed-baffle response.
