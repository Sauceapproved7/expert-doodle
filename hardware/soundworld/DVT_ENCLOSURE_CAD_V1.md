# SoundWorld V1 — DVT Enclosure / CAD Architecture
Status: CAD constraint specification; final industrial-design dimensions remain unfrozen.

## Acoustic geometry
Preserve the 6.0–8.5 L gross study envelope. Left/right mid-bass and tweeter geometry remains acoustically symmetric unless measurements justify departure. Passive radiators remain mechanically opposed. Drivers/radiators receive dedicated gasket lands.

## Internal zones
Separate acoustic, battery, power-electronics, low-level digital/RF and USB-C zones. Battery and PCB assemblies require vibration isolation and positive retention. Wiring cannot enter moving driver/radiator clearance volumes.

## RF
Reserve at least 10 mm preliminary antenna keep-out from conductive/high-current structures; final geometry follows selected antenna guidance plus chamber/range testing. Metal cosmetics cannot cross the RF window before validation.

## Impact architecture
Maintain at least 5 mm preliminary protected clearance around vulnerable internals where packaging permits. Drop loads route into structural ribs/shell, not battery cells, PCB edges, USB-C connector or driver baskets.

## Sealing
Continuous gasket strategy around enclosure split, drivers, radiators and service openings. Compression stops control gasket compression. No ingress claim before production-equivalent test.

## Serviceability
Battery and USB-C subassembly are replaceable without destructive enclosure separation where practical. Service opening must restore intended sealing with a defined gasket/fastener procedure.

## CAD freeze inputs
Exact driver/PR CAD, battery envelope plus swelling allowance, PCB outline/connectors, antenna keep-out, thermal interfaces, fastener bosses, grille deflection clearance, gasket data and measured component displacement.

## CAD freeze evidence
Interference/clearance review, tolerance stack, modal/panel review, drop engineering assessment, thermal review, RF review, gasket compression analysis, assembly/disassembly trial and updated net-volume calculation.
