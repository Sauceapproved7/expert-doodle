## Controlled critical characteristics
External envelope; acoustic net volume; driver/tweeter symmetry; driver flange/opening after exact supplier CAD import; PR opening and full sweep clearance; battery cassette envelope/retention; antenna keep-out; service disconnect access; shell/baffle study thickness; gasket lands/compression; fastener boss geometry; handle load path; harness pinch/abrasion clearance.

## Tolerance policy
Do not invent production tolerances from the study model. Prototype drawings mark functional interfaces as CTQ and derive tolerances from actual supplier drawings, prototype process capability and measured fit. Noncritical cosmetic geometry may use prototype-house standard tolerances only when recorded on the quote/drawing.

## Materials
Prototype shell/baffle material remains an engineering selection, not a marketing claim. Material lot/process must be recorded. Acoustic/structural comparison may use more than one prototype material only when unit configuration clearly identifies it.

## Fasteners and seals
Fastener schedule records type, material, thread, engagement, torque study value and locking method. Gasket schedule records material, cross-section, compression target, adhesive if any, service replacement status and revision.

## Revision/deviation control
Every drawing carries part number, revision, date and approver. Builder deviations require written deviation ID before incorporation. Any change affecting acoustic volume, transducer geometry, battery retention, RF keep-out, thermal path, sealing, protection or service access triggers regression review.

## Incoming/fabrication inspection
Record CTQ dimensions; visual defects; insert/boss integrity; gasket land flatness; service-panel fit; battery retention fit; PR sweep clearance; driver flange fit; handle/chassis fit; mass and center-of-mass baseline.

## Release boundary
No injection-mold tooling, production machining, retail packaging, compliance mark or public performance claim is authorized.
