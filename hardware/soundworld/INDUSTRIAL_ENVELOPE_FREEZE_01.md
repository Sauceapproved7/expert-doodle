## Original SoundWorld physical language
Horizontal monolithic body with a protected central spine. End structures act as impact guards around the opposed radiators without becoming acoustic obstructions. A structural carry bridge/handle mounts into chassis load paths rather than speaker baffle panels. Feet create stable four-point support and protect the lower shell and USB-C service region.

## Control surface
Top-center tactile control island: power, volume down/up, Scene Mode, Hercules Link. Status indication is restrained and readable; no always-listening microphone is required by the architecture.

## Impact/sealing
Minimum 5 mm study clearance from outer impact shell to protected internal critical components where geometry permits. Battery retention is structural, not cosmetic. Port/service seals are replaceable parts. Gasket compression and fastener torque become controlled drawing parameters.

## Mass and balance
Battery raw cells contribute ~0.55 kg before cassette/BMS. Final center of mass must stay inside the support polygon in normal orientations and be measured on EVT hardware. Handle proof load and drop loading remain physical validation gates.

## Acoustic geometry
2 × front-facing mid-bass drivers and 2 × front-facing tweeters arranged symmetrically; 2 × opposed side passive radiators. Final baffle spacing, edge treatment, chamber bracing, damping and crossover remain measurement-correlated parameters.
