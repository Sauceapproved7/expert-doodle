# SoundWorld Wearables Platform V1

## Products
1. SoundWorld Pods — premium true-wireless earbuds + owned charging-case system.
2. SoundWorld Max — premium over-ear wireless headphones.

Both are original SauceApproved system designs. Specialist drivers, microphones, cells and semiconductors are qualified components.

## Premium baseline
ANC; transparency/ambient mode; beamformed voice calling; spatial processing/head tracking where validated; multipoint/device switching; USB-C charging; battery telemetry; find/status support where platform permissions allow; signed firmware; rollback-safe updates; app-based EQ and controls.

## Hercules differentiator 1 — Personal Sound DNA
Optional local-first calibration profile describing preferred tonal balance, safe loudness behavior, scene preferences and device-specific compensation. One abstract profile maps separately onto Pods, Max and Speaker rather than copying raw EQ coefficients between different acoustics. Export/delete/reset are explicit. No medical/hearing-health claim.

## Hercules differentiator 2 — SoundWorld Continuity
Owned session-state layer that can transfer supported playback intent between SoundWorld devices: Scene Mode, relative loudness intent, Sound DNA profile, spatial preference and device role. Transport/content-provider restrictions are respected; it does not bypass DRM or OS permissions.

## Safety authority
Hardware electrical/thermal protection > firmware hearing/loudness safety > ANC/acoustic protection > DSP calibration > Scene Mode > Sound DNA > Continuity. User features cannot override hard safety limits.

## SoundWorld Pods architecture study
Sealed ergonomic earbud; custom acoustic chamber/nozzle study; dynamic-driver baseline subject to measured selection; feedforward + feedback ANC microphones plus call microphones; per-bud battery/protection/temperature telemetry; owned charging case with USB-C; independent left/right signed firmware identity; wear/touch controls; no always-on cloud requirement.

## SoundWorld Max architecture study
Closed-back over-ear baseline; custom earcup acoustic volume and baffle; large dynamic-driver study; multi-mic hybrid ANC/call array; replaceable/serviceable ear cushions; internal battery module designed for service; tactile controls; USB-C charging and wired-audio fallback subject to interface study.

## Shared software
SoundWorld device identity; signed firmware/update manifest; Sound DNA schema; Continuity state schema; Scene Mode mapping; diagnostic telemetry; factory calibration record; fail-closed update/rollback; privacy-local profile storage by default.

## Evidence gates
No ANC depth, battery-hours, SPL, frequency response, microphone quality, latency, ingress, spatial accuracy or comparative performance claim until physical EVT measurement.
