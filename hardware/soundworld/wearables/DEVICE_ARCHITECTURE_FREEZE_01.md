# SoundWorld Pods + Max — Device Architecture Freeze 01

## Pods
- Independent left/right secure device identity and rollback-safe firmware.
- Dynamic-driver acoustic study; exact driver waits for measured candidate qualification.
- Hybrid ANC topology: external feed-forward + internal feedback sensing, plus dedicated voice path.
- Owned charging case: USB-C input, protected case battery, per-bud charging supervision, thermal/fault telemetry.
- Wear sensing and tactile/touch control study.
- Sound DNA maps abstract preference/safe-loudness intent into bud-specific DSP calibration.
- Continuity preserves supported Scene/profile/listening state across SoundWorld devices.
- Hearing/loudness protection outranks EQ, Scene Mode and personalization.
- Exact battery cells, RF SoC/audio codec, microphones and driver remain qualification items.

## Max
- Closed-back circumaural acoustic study with custom baffle/earcup volume; exact driver waits for measured qualification.
- Hybrid multi-mic ANC and dedicated call beamforming path.
- Serviceable ear cushions and service-oriented internal battery module.
- USB-C charging; wired fallback retained as an engineering requirement pending analog/USB interface freeze.
- Tactile controls for core functions.
- Shared signed firmware, Sound DNA and Continuity schemas.
- Hearing/loudness and thermal protection outrank user DSP.
- Exact driver, battery, RF/audio silicon and microphone array remain qualification items.

## Measurement gates
For both products, EVT must measure frequency response, distortion, max safe SPL, ANC attenuation vs frequency, transparency response, microphone intelligibility/noise, RF stability, latency, battery runtime, charge temperature, enclosure/fit effects and firmware rollback.

No battery-life, ANC-depth, sound-quality superiority, latency, ingress or comparative claim is authorized from this architecture.
