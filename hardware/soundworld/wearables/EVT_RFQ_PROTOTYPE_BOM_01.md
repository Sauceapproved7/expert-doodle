# SoundWorld Pods + Max — EVT RFQ / Prototype BOM 01

## RFQ scope
Quote only. No purchase, fabrication order, tooling, production, certification or customer shipment is authorized.

Prototype quantities:
- SoundWorld Pods: 5 complete left/right/case engineering sets.
- SoundWorld Max: 3 complete engineering units.
Include sensible engineering spares as separately priced lines.

## Pods prototype BOM classes
- QCC7226/Snapdragon S7 Gen 1-class audio platform/reference implementation, commercial-access dependent.
- 2 micro-dynamic drivers per complete set (one/bud), exact MPN supplier-proposed against owned acoustic envelope and then SauceApproved-qualified.
- TDK T5837 microphone evaluation baseline; quantities/positions according to ANC + voice prototype topology, exact final mic MPN qualification-controlled.
- 2 miniature protected rechargeable cells per set plus spares; supplier must provide exact manufacturer/cell model, dimensions, rated/typical capacity, charge/discharge limits, cycle data, safety/transport evidence and traceability.
- Case battery, protected charging/power electronics, USB-C receptacle/protection, per-bud charging contacts, thermal sensors.
- Wear sensors, antenna/RF implementation, flex/rigid-flex/PCB, tactile/touch interfaces.
- Prototype shells, nozzles, vents, tips, seals and acoustic mesh.
- Case Guardian storage/control/recovery support hardware where required by final partition.

## Max prototype BOM classes
- QCC7226/S7-class platform/reference implementation.
- 2 large dynamic driver candidates per unit, exact MPN frozen after owned-earcup measurements.
- T5837 evaluation microphones or qualified alternate array.
- Protected service-oriented rechargeable battery assembly.
- USB-C charge/protection; wired/USB audio interface parts.
- Antenna/RF implementation, PCB/flex, thermal sensors, tactile controls.
- Prototype headband/yokes/cups/baffles/cushions/seals/fasteners.
- Acoustic damping/vent materials tracked by exact lot/spec because they affect Acoustic Twin calibration.

## Supplier response required for every critical line
Manufacturer; exact MPN; authorized source; lifecycle status; MOQ; unit price; lead time; country of origin if available; datasheet/revision; compliance documentation; lot/date-code traceability; substitution policy; sample availability.

No substitution is automatically accepted. Any proposed alternate is a new qualification candidate.

## Hold points
H0 supplier/MPN review.
H1 acoustic-driver and microphone evidence review.
H2 battery safety/transport evidence.
H3 RF/reference-design review.
H4 mechanical/CAD review.
H5 owner purchase authorization.
H6 incoming inspection.
H7 current-limited/controlled first power.
H8 EVT acceptance matrix.

## Feature-specific prototype evidence
Pods: Private Scene Mesh + Case Guardian must be included in EVT firmware/hardware planning.
Max: Acoustic Twin + Creator Monitor must be included in EVT calibration/firmware/interface planning.

## Claim boundary
Prototype quotes and supplier datasheets do not prove finished-product battery life, ANC depth, sound quality, latency, ingress, durability or superiority.
