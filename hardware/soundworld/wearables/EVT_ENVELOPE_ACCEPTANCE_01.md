# SoundWorld Pods + Max — EVT Envelope and Acceptance 01

## Pods EVT industrial envelope
Engineering mass target: <= 6 g per bud before final ergonomic validation.
Compact sealed in-ear body with replaceable tip interface, controlled nozzle/vent geometry, external ANC reference port, internal feedback microphone path, dedicated voice pickup geometry, wear sensor, protected cell zone and antenna keep-out.
Charging case is an active service/recovery device for Case Guardian, not only a battery container.
Final shell dimensions remain parametric until driver, cell, antenna and anthropometric fit stack closes.

## Max EVT industrial envelope
Engineering mass target: <= 300 g before final structural/acoustic validation.
Circumaural closed-back architecture with replaceable cushions, isolated driver/baffle assembly, microphone apertures protected from handling turbulence, service-oriented battery region, tactile control island and wired/USB interface region.
Headband clamp force, cup depth, yoke travel and cushion geometry remain parametric until head-fixture and wear trials.

## EVT acceptance — Pods
Must pass: frequency response/THD/compression on ear simulator; ANC attenuation by frequency; transparency response; voice intelligibility/noise/wind tests; RF coexistence; charge and discharge thermal tests; measured runtime; fit/seal repeatability; signed update/rollback; Private Scene Mesh privacy/schema tests; Case Guardian recovery and battery-health tests.

## EVT acceptance — Max
Must pass: head-fixture FR/THD/compression; ANC/transparency; call beamforming; wired/USB latency; RF coexistence; thermal/runtime; cushion/seal perturbation; signed update/rollback; Acoustic Twin repeatability/recalibration; Creator Monitor neutral-path response and latency.

## Competitive target handling
Published competitor battery/runtime figures are benchmarking inputs only. SoundWorld public runtime, ANC depth, SPL, latency, frequency-response, ingress and superiority claims require measured SoundWorld hardware evidence.

EVT acceptance is an engineering milestone only. It does not authorize production, certification or sale.
