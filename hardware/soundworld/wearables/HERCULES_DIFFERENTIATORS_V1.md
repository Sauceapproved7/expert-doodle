# SoundWorld Wearables — Hercules Differentiators V1

Competitor feature review date: 2026-09-29.
Reviewed public feature sets from Apple AirPods Pro 3 / AirPods Max 2, Sony premium WF/WH class, Bose QuietComfort Ultra class, and Samsung Galaxy Buds3 Pro class. Baseline capabilities such as ANC, adaptive/transparency modes, conversation detection, personalized EQ/volume, spatial audio/head tracking, translation, hearing features, multipoint/seamless switching, and ordinary charging are not treated as Hercules-exclusive.

## SoundWorld Pods — two product-specific additions

### 1. Private Scene Mesh
A consent-based, local-first cooperative awareness layer between nearby SoundWorld devices. Participating devices exchange only bounded acoustic-event classes/confidence—not raw microphone audio—to improve awareness of selected events across a group (for example a family, crew, or training group). Each wearer independently controls participation and event classes. It never bypasses hard loudness/hearing protection and is not a safety-certified emergency system.

Why it is distinct from baseline: this is not ordinary transparency, conversation detection, or phone-based translation. It is an owned privacy-bounded multi-device acoustic context protocol.

### 2. Case Guardian
The charging case becomes an offline integrity/maintenance controller rather than a passive battery box. It verifies bud firmware identity before coordinated updates, maintains a signed recovery image, tracks left/right battery-health imbalance, performs bounded acoustic/self-test scheduling while docked, and can restore a failed bud to the last approved firmware image. No cloud connection is required for core recovery.

Why it is distinct from baseline: this combines case-level firmware recovery, pair integrity and predictive left/right maintenance into the owned case architecture.

## SoundWorld Max — two product-specific additions

### 1. Acoustic Twin
Each physical Max unit receives a factory acoustic fingerprint tied to its serial identity. On-device calibration tracks seal/cushion-state drift against that fingerprint and applies bounded compensation. After an approved cushion replacement, a guided local recalibration creates a new signed calibration state without erasing the factory reference.

Why it is distinct from baseline: it treats the individual physical headphone plus replaceable wear parts as a maintained calibrated system, not merely a user EQ profile.

### 2. Creator Monitor
A hardware/software monitoring path for creators that can bypass entertainment spatialization and Sound DNA coloration, loads a signed neutral-monitor calibration, exposes latency/calibration state, and lets a creator A/B the neutral reference against their personal listening profile without changing the master profile. USB/wired transport remains preferred where validated for deterministic latency.

Why it is distinct from baseline: it separates entertainment personalization from a traceable neutral-reference workflow for editing/mixing/review.

## Claim boundary
These are original Hercules design differentiators selected after a current feature review. The review did not establish that no competitor anywhere has an analogous feature. Therefore marketing may describe the concrete capabilities, but must not say globally unique, first ever, or that competitors do not have them without a broader documented patent/product search.
