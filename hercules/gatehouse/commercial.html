HOW DOMAIN VERIFICATION WILL WORK

Your site. Your permission. Your proof.

Gatehouse is developing a verification method that asks an authorized website owner to publish a short, unique challenge at a safe /.well-known/sauceapproved-gatehouse/ path. After authentication and consent, Gatehouse would check only that challenge using certificate-verified HTTPS and a pinned public IP address.
