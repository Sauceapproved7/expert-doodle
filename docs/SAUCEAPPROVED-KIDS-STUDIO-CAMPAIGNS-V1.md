Status: creative copy ready for review. Nothing is scheduled, posted, or sold by this file. Destination: the owned Studio `/kids` page. All three kits remain available to every child regardless of campaign angle.

## Three 15-second vertical scripts

**Hero — parent audience**

0–4s: A toy walks through a paper neighborhood. On-screen: “Every hero starts somewhere.”

4–10s: The viewfinder shows “Ask what they need” and then an alternate ending. Voice: “Help them direct a story where kindness changes the scene.”

10–15s: The three-shot plan appears. Voice and CTA: “Make one together in SauceApproved Kids Studio.” Small disclosure: “Parent-operated. Plan saves on your device.”

**Director — parent audience**

0–4s: A handmade curtain opens. On-screen: “Your kid has a vision.”

4–10s: A silent three-beat board and two camera angles animate. Voice: “Let them call the shots, set the rhythm, and try both sides of the scene.”

10–15s: Vintage dial lands on Dream Director. Voice and CTA: “Build their first shot plan together.” Small disclosure: “Parent-operated. No public posting.”

**Capsule — family audience**

0–4s: A blank cassette label reads “Today.” On-screen: “The little things become the big memories.”

4–10s: Show a drawing, a favorite object, and a future question. Voice: “Capture a moment now and leave a question for later.”

10–15s: Privacy Check flags a synthetic email and the parent removes it. Voice and CTA: “Start a Time Capsule in Kids Studio.” Small disclosure: “Review before saving or sharing.”

## Production notes

- Make initial ads with illustrated props, graphics, or licensed adult-performed footage. Do not use real child images or voices without appropriate permission and a separate review of the distribution path.
- Direct the CTA to adults. Avoid collecting a child’s name, age, location, image, or voice in a marketing form.
- Show actual product behavior. The kits create local text shot plans; the separate vintage camera captures locally when supported. Do not depict automatic AI video generation or cloud editing.
- Produce 9:16 cutdowns with captions and a silent first frame that carries the hook. Keep the readable CTA and disclosure on screen long enough to understand.
- Measure visits to `/kids` and adult-directed interest through approved analytics only after the privacy and commercial controls for that measurement are set. No tracking is enabled by this pack.
