# Hercules Launch Positioning

## Core promise

**Recover cash. Keep control. Prove every action.**

Hercules Revenue Recovery is the first customer product on the Hercules operating system. It helps a business turn receivables evidence into a safe next step without hiding the reasoning or taking uncontrolled action.

## What the customer should understand in 30 seconds

1. Hercules identifies which receivables need attention.
2. It explains why each case is in its current state.
3. It separates safe follow-up from disputes, promises to pay, paid evidence, and human-review cases.
4. Consequential actions remain approval-gated.
5. Hercules preserves evidence for what it saw, decided, and changed.

## Differentiation

Hercules is not positioned as another reminder scheduler. The product centers:
- evidence-backed recovery cases;
- explicit reason codes and current-state authority;
- owner approval boundaries;
- consequence and recovery evidence;
- verifiable action history;
- a shared Hercules trust runtime underneath customer workflows.

## Claims discipline

Do not claim guaranteed collections, guaranteed savings, recovery probabilities, credit scoring, legal collection services, or autonomous debt collection.

Do not present staging benchmarks as production uptime history.

## Brand standard

Customer-facing Hercules should feel deliberate, premium, controlled, and unmistakably Hercules. Avoid generic AI gradients, fake activity, invented metrics, and template filler. Product polish must not outrun evidence.
