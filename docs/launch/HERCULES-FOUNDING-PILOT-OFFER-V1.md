# Hercules Revenue Recovery — Founding Pilot Offer v1

**Status:** Packaged and executable; commercial activation remains owner/release gated.  
**Product:** Hercules Revenue Recovery  
**Initial market:** U.S. businesses managing their own commercial receivables.

## Offer

**Hercules Revenue Recovery — Founding Pilot**

A controlled evaluation for businesses that have overdue B2B receivables and need a safer way to decide what happens next. Hercules separates ordinary follow-up candidates from disputes, active payment promises, paid evidence, do-not-contact conditions, unverified history, and other human-review cases.

The pilot is built around one measurable product milestone: **First verified useful action**. It does not promise that an invoice will be paid or guarantee a recovery outcome.

## Target customer

Strong initial fit:
- agencies;
- contractors;
- consultants and professional-services firms;
- bookkeeping/accounting firms;
- wholesalers;
- commercial-service businesses.

Primary buyer/operator roles:
- owner or founder;
- finance / accounts receivable;
- accountant or bookkeeper;
- fractional CFO or consultant.

A pilot is in scope only when the business is managing **its own commercial receivables**.

## Painful problem

Traditional reminder workflows can send the wrong message at the wrong time because they treat every late invoice as equivalent. That becomes risky when an invoice is disputed, a payment promise is still active, the balance is already paid, contact should stop, or the history is incomplete.

Hercules starts with the current evidence, identifies the case state, and only permits follow-up when the case is actually contact-ready.

## Pilot deliverables

1. **Recovery Desk workspace** — authenticated access to the live customer-facing Revenue Recovery workspace.
2. **Supported receivable intake** — capture the source/invoice reference, amount, age, available contact data, and current case state.
3. **Case-state routing** — visibly distinguish contact-ready work from blocked/human-review states.
4. **Approval-gated follow-up plan** — only permitted channels can be queued, and consequential actions remain behind authorization boundaries.
5. **Human-review protection** — disputed, active-promise, paid, do-not-contact, unverified-history, and manual-review cases cannot queue external follow-up.
6. **Verified action history** — retain what Hercules saw, the route it chose, approval state, and recorded result.
7. **Pilot readout** — review what happened, where Hercules added useful control, and whether the customer should convert to a paid plan.

## Intake-to-delivery workflow

### 1. Pilot intake
The existing public founding-pilot form records the business contact request into the protected Hercules marketing system.

### 2. Qualification
Confirm:
- the company manages its own B2B receivables;
- the use case is not consumer or third-party debt collection;
- the customer accepts approval-gated external actions;
- the evaluation can begin with synthetic data if real customer data is not yet authorized.

### 3. Workspace activation
Provide an authorized Hercules workspace only through the existing launch/account controls. General public registration remains fail-closed until the launch gate is satisfied.

### 4. First receivable
Add the first supported receivable to Recovery Desk with its real or authorized source reference and current case state. Never invent invoice, payment, dispute, or promise facts.

### 5. Route and review
Hercules determines whether the case is contact-ready or must remain blocked for human review. Unsupported or unsafe actions are unavailable rather than simulated.

### 6. Verified useful action
Reach the first evidence-backed useful state: a safe next route, blocked-state decision, recorded outcome, or other verified customer value supported by the live product.

### 7. Pilot readout
Show the customer the case trail, action boundaries, recorded results, and any gaps that would block broader use.

### 8. Conversion decision
If the pilot demonstrates value, move to an owner-approved commercial plan. No billing is activated by this document.

## Commercial structure

The commercial model is a **monthly software subscription**, not an outcome-based collection fee.

Current post-pilot proposal:
- **Starter:** $99/month.

That figure remains a **proposal only** until the owner approves pricing and the commercial launch gates are complete. No founding-pilot fee is active yet. A customer must not be represented as owing a pilot or subscription payment solely because this package exists.

The higher Growth and Scale proposals remain available for later use but are intentionally outside this first narrow offer.

## Data boundary

Default pilot mode is **synthetic until authorized**.

Real customer data may be used only after explicit authorization and the applicable production data-handling requirements are satisfied.

## External-action boundary

Consequential external actions remain **approval gated**. The pilot does not authorize Hercules to perform uncontrolled autonomous collection activity.

## Explicit exclusions

The founding pilot is not offered as:
- consumer debt collection;
- third-party debt collection;
- credit scoring;
- debt purchasing;
- legal collections;
- guaranteed recovery;
- uncontrolled autonomous collection.

## What is live now

This offer depends only on:
- `hercules-launch` for the launch/intake/workspace surface; and
- `hercules-revenue-rescue` for the Recovery Desk backend.

The offer does not require speculative integrations or unfinished future features.
