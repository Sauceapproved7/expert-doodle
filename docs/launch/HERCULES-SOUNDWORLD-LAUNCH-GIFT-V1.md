# Hercules First 14 Days SoundWorld Gift

## Owner directive

For the first 14 calendar days after Hercules public paid launch opens, every qualifying Hercules customer purchase earns one promotional SoundWorld gift at $0 product price.

The customer chooses exactly one:

- SoundWorld Pods
- SoundWorld Max
- SoundWorld portable speaker

## Launch clock

The promotion does not start on the date this document is created. The 14-day window begins at the recorded `public_paid_launch_opened_at` timestamp so pre-launch payment gating does not consume promotional days.

## Qualification

A qualifying purchase is a successfully settled real-customer purchase of a Hercules paid offer that is publicly enabled during the launch window. Internal verification charges and test transactions do not qualify.

One gift reservation is permitted per qualifying purchase. The reservation must be bound to the purchase and customer identity so the same transaction cannot claim multiple gifts.

## Fulfillment

SoundWorld hardware is still pre-production. A qualifying purchase creates a $0 promotional reservation for the selected product. Physical fulfillment occurs after the selected SoundWorld product clears EVT and production availability.

The delayed physical-fulfillment status must be disclosed before the customer completes purchase. The promotion must not imply that production hardware is ready to ship when it is not.

## Commercial controls

This promotion:

- does not change the base Hercules purchase price;
- does not enable checkout by itself;
- does not bypass Pricing, Terms, Privacy, payment-provider, or payment-path verification gates;
- must remain fail-closed until the underlying Hercules purchase is verified;
- must preserve one-gift-per-qualifying-purchase enforcement.

Canonical machine-readable packet: `governance/hercules-soundworld-launch-gift-v1.json`.
