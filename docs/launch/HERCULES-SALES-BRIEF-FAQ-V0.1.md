# Hercules Revenue Recovery — Sales Brief + FAQ v0.1

## One-sentence brief

Hercules Revenue Recovery helps a business turn overdue B2B receivables evidence into a controlled, explainable next action with approvals, verification, and proof.

## Who it is for

Initial fit:
- agencies;
- contractors;
- consultants;
- professional-services firms;
- bookkeeping/accounting firms;
- wholesalers;
- commercial-service businesses.

## Why Hercules

Traditional reminder automation can treat every late invoice as the same problem. Hercules starts with current evidence and separates routine overdue cases from disputes, promises to pay, paid evidence, and human-review situations.

## What happens

1. Ingest supported receivable evidence.
2. Establish current invoice state.
3. Produce a reasoned recovery route.
4. Gate consequential actions for approval.
5. Verify the result.
6. Preserve an evidence trail.

## Pilot offer

Founding pilots are controlled evaluations focused on the first verified useful action rather than a promised recovery outcome.

Use synthetic data until real customer data is explicitly authorized.

## FAQ

### Does Hercules guarantee payment recovery?
No. Hercules does not promise or guarantee collections outcomes.

### Is Hercules a debt collector?
The initial product is designed for businesses managing their own commercial receivables. Third-party and consumer debt-collection use is outside the initial launch scope.

### Does Hercules act without approval?
Consequential external actions are designed to remain behind approval and authorization boundaries.

### Is it a credit-scoring product?
No. The Revenue Recovery relationship graph does not create credit scores, collectability scores, or recovery probabilities.

### What does "prove every action" mean?
Hercules preserves inspectable evidence of the facts it used, the route it proposed, the relevant approval state, and the verified result.

### What customer data is used in demos?
Public demos use synthetic data. Real customer data should be used only after authorization and production data-handling requirements are met.

### Is pricing final?
No. Current pricing material is a proposal until owner approval, billing configuration, and commercial launch gates are complete.

### Are Terms and Privacy final?
No. Prepared drafts remain pending owner/qualified review and must match the actual live configuration before general availability.
