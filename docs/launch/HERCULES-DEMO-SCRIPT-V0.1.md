# Hercules Revenue Recovery — Demo Script v0.1

## 60–90 second proof

**0–10 sec — Problem**
Three invoices are overdue. One is routine, one is disputed, one has a payment promise. Blind reminder automation would risk treating them alike.

**10–25 sec — Evidence**
Show the synthetic evidence set and source-preserved case facts.

**25–40 sec — State**
Hercules establishes each current state and explains the reason.

**40–55 sec — Route**
Show different safe routes: controlled follow-up, human review, and hold-until-promise-window.

**55–70 sec — Approval**
Show that consequential action remains approval-gated.

**70–90 sec — Proof**
Reach `first_verified_useful_action` and show the proof trail. Do not claim an external collection action occurred.

## Five-minute workflow

1. Import supported synthetic receivable evidence.
2. Inspect current-state determination and reason codes.
3. Inspect the smallest safe next route.
4. Review the approval boundary.
5. Verify the useful action and retained evidence.

## Demo dataset

- Invoice A: $8,400, 31 days overdue, no dispute, no payment promise.
- Invoice B: $6,000, 18 days overdue, disputed line items.
- Invoice C: $4,000, 12 days overdue, payment promised for Friday.

Total synthetic balance: $18,400.

## Claims rule

The demo proves workflow behavior only. It does not prove recovery rate, production uptime, legal compliance across all jurisdictions, or customer savings.
