# Hercules Revenue Recovery — Launch Pricing Proposal

This document is a product proposal, not active billing configuration.

## Starter — $99/month
For small owner-operated businesses testing a structured recovery workflow.
- 1 workspace
- core recovery cases
- owner approval workflow
- evidence/reason history
- standard usage allowance

## Growth — $249/month
For businesses with a larger receivables operation.
- expanded usage allowance
- multiple workspace members
- advanced recovery history
- priority operational support
- broader supported integrations as they become verified

## Scale — $599/month
For higher-volume teams that need stronger operational controls.
- higher usage allowance
- advanced workspace roles
- export/audit workflows
- deployment/integration support
- higher-touch onboarding

## Commercial guardrails

- No outcome-based collection fee at launch unless legal/compliance review explicitly approves the model.
- Do not charge for a capability that is not live.
- Usage limits shown in-product must come from the same entitlement source enforced by Hercules.
- Payment-provider status is not proof of Hercules authorization; entitlement changes must be verified server-side.
- Prices remain configurable until the owner activates a billing provider and final launch offer.
