# Hercules Launch Support Policy

## Support levels

### Starter
Standard product support for account access, supported workflow questions, and verified product defects.

### Growth
Priority support for workflow/integration issues and recovery-case investigation.

### Scale
Higher-touch onboarding and operational support for supported deployments/integrations.

## Incident priority

- **P1:** security incident, cross-tenant exposure, widespread production outage, or destructive execution risk.
- **P2:** major paid workflow unavailable with no reasonable workaround.
- **P3:** degraded feature, integration problem, or customer-specific operational issue.
- **P4:** product question, cosmetic issue, or enhancement request.

## Launch prerequisites

Before public launch the operator must configure:
- a monitored customer support channel;
- a monitored security reporting channel;
- ownership for incident response;
- status/incident communication process;
- retention of relevant proof/audit evidence.

Do not publish response-time guarantees until the staffing/monitoring process can actually meet them.
