# Hercules Legal and Privacy Launch Review

This is an implementation checklist, not legal advice and not a substitute for counsel.

## Business/legal items requiring owner or qualified review

- legal entity name and contracting party;
- Terms of Service;
- Privacy Policy;
- data-retention/deletion commitments;
- subprocessors and third-party service disclosures;
- payment/billing terms and refund/cancellation policy;
- acceptable-use restrictions;
- security/incident notice language;
- intellectual-property terms;
- jurisdiction, governing law, and dispute terms;
- receivables/collections-law review for the exact launch behavior;
- marketing claims review;
- tax/accounting treatment;
- any outcome-based fee model before activation.

## Product/privacy facts to document accurately

- categories of account/workspace data;
- receivable/invoice data handled;
- message/audit/proof evidence retained;
- authentication/session mechanisms;
- provider adapters/subprocessors actually used;
- deletion/export process;
- retention periods actually enforced in code/configuration;
- customer role/tenant isolation controls;
- whether model/provider data is retained or used for training, based on the actual provider configuration.

## Release rule

Legal/privacy documents must describe the product that is actually live. Never copy generic clauses that promise controls Hercules has not implemented.

Public launch should remain blocked on any legally required owner signature, identity verification, payment account approval, or counsel review that cannot be completed by repository code.
