## Creation boundary

LinkedIn requires a real personal LinkedIn account to create and administer a Company Page. The owner must personally complete the LinkedIn account login, applicable verification, and confirmation that they are authorized to act for the company.

Do not create a personal-profile account named SauceApproved. LinkedIn profiles represent real people; the business belongs on a Company Page.
