# SauceApproved Software Privacy Policy — FINAL CANDIDATE v1

**Status:** FINAL CANDIDATE — OWNER/QUALIFIED REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE EFFECTIVE DATE  
**Company:** SauceApproved enterprise LLC  
**Products:** SauceApproved Studio (AI Video Maker) and SauceApproved Ads (AI Ad Maker)

This candidate describes the intended launch privacy model for these products. It is **not effective** until SauceApproved enterprise LLC affirmatively approves and publishes it.

## 1. Scope

This policy is intended to cover information processed when a customer:
- visits the Studio or Ads product surfaces;
- requests founding access;
- creates or uses an account;
- joins a workspace;
- creates campaigns, videos, storyboards, ad concepts, or related assets;
- uploads or connects media, brand, campaign, or project information;
- uses AI-assisted or rendering features;
- contacts support;
- uses a paid plan if billing is activated.

It does not govern a third party's independent service or website.

## 2. Information we may process

### Account and identity information
Examples include email address, account identifier, authentication/session information, workspace membership, role, and authorization evidence.

### Product and workspace information
Examples include project names, campaign names, brand settings, plan and entitlement state, usage state, connected-provider configuration, and workspace settings.

### Studio content
SauceApproved Studio may process prompts, scripts, storyboards, shot plans, uploaded media, generated or transformed media, captions, rendering instructions, quality-review results, provenance records, and export packages.

### Ads content
SauceApproved Ads may process campaign briefs, product or offer information, messaging angles, creative text, uploaded media, generated creative, UTM parameters, budget-planning data, experiment records, and manually entered performance information.

### Support, security, and audit information
We may process support messages, approval history, evidence records, integrity digests, authentication and authorization events, security events, release records, service-health information, and incident records.

### Usage and technical information
Examples include feature usage, request timestamps, usage counters, logs, latency, errors, device/browser information, and network information made available by infrastructure providers.

### Billing information
If paid billing is activated, Hercules may receive customer, subscription, invoice, plan, payment-status, refund, and payout-state identifiers from **Stripe** or another approved payment processor.

Payment-card details should be collected and handled by the payment processor rather than stored directly by SauceApproved, unless the production architecture is explicitly changed and reviewed.

## 3. How information is used

Information may be used to:
- provide and operate SauceApproved Studio and SauceApproved Ads;
- authenticate users and enforce permissions;
- generate, transform, render, assemble, analyze, or export requested content;
- apply plan and usage limits;
- secure the products and investigate abuse or incidents;
- maintain audit, provenance, recovery, and release evidence;
- provide support;
- process billing when activated;
- comply with legal obligations;
- improve supported product features as permitted by applicable law and the final customer agreement.

## 4. AI and rendering providers

Some features may send selected customer inputs or media to AI, rendering, or processing providers when the customer invokes those features.

The exact production provider list may change as Hercules routes work through approved adapters. Before this policy becomes effective, the current provider list and material data flows must be verified against production.

SauceApproved does not claim that every provider follows one universal retention or training policy. Provider-specific terms and privacy policies may apply to downstream processing.

## 5. Infrastructure and subprocessors

Verified or intended launch infrastructure currently includes:

- **Supabase** — authentication, database, protected product catalog, access-request storage, entitlement state, serverless functions, audit/evidence records, and release controls;
- **Render** — presentation hosting for the Hercules-owned Studio and Ads release surfaces;
- **GitHub** — source control, CI/security/provenance checks, and release/source evidence;
- **Stripe** — intended payment processing for paid subscriptions after commercial activation and verification.

Additional AI or rendering providers must be documented before they are treated as active subprocessors for the final published policy.

## 6. Founding-access requests

The public founding-access form collects only bounded business contact and request information.

Direct browser access to the protected request table is disabled. Requests are written through a constrained server-side database function with validation, honeypot handling, and rate limits.

## 7. Payment processing

Paid checkout remains disabled until:
- product pricing is approved;
- these Terms are approved;
- this Privacy Policy is approved;
- the payment provider is verified;
- the checkout, subscription, cancellation/refund, and entitlement path is verified.

When Stripe is activated, SauceApproved should store only the billing identifiers and state needed to operate subscriptions and customer access. Raw payment-card details should remain with Stripe.

## 8. Data retention

Retention periods depend on the type of information, product function, customer relationship, legal requirements, security needs, and active provider behavior.

SauceApproved should not publish a universal retention period unless production behavior actually enforces it.

Before final publication, retention disclosures must match the live Studio/Ads architecture and any active AI/render providers.

## 9. Security

SauceApproved uses technical and operational measures intended to reduce risk, including access controls, server-side authorization, protected request storage, release gates, monitoring, provenance checks, and recovery systems.

No security measure can eliminate all risk.

## 10. Customer responsibilities

Customers should not upload information that is unnecessary for their intended workflow.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that they have the authority to provide media, personal information, brand assets, advertising data, client data, likenesses, trademarks, or other content they submit.

## 11. International and regional processing

Infrastructure or subprocessors may process information in locations outside the customer's state or country.

Before final publication, SauceApproved must confirm any jurisdiction-specific disclosures required for the intended launch markets.

## 12. Privacy rights

Depending on applicable law and the location of the customer or data subject, rights may include access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or appeal.

The final policy must identify a monitored method for submitting privacy requests and must match the production request-handling process.

## 13. Children's data

SauceApproved Studio and SauceApproved Ads are business/creator software products and are not directed to children.

Customers must not knowingly use the products to collect or process children's personal information in violation of applicable law.

## 14. Changes

The final published policy must state its effective date and explain how material changes are communicated.

## 15. Contact

Privacy and support requests should use a monitored SauceApproved business channel or in-product request center. Personal contact information should not be published as the default business contact unless the owner expressly chooses to do so.
