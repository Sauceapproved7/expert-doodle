## 3. Exclusions and preserved rights

This assignment does **not** transfer or claim ownership of:

- third-party software, services, libraries, runtimes, models, datasets, infrastructure, media, fonts, binaries, or other third-party material;
- rights the Assignor does not own or cannot lawfully assign;
- rights previously and validly granted to others under Apache License 2.0 or any other applicable prior license;
- trademarks, domains, patents, accounts, contracts, regulated licenses, or other assets unless they are expressly listed in Schedule A or a later signed addendum.

Any third-party material remains governed by its own license and ownership terms.

## 4. Further assurances

Assignor agrees to execute reasonable additional documents needed to confirm the assignment of the rights described here, provided those documents do not expand the transfer beyond the intended scope without separate agreement.
