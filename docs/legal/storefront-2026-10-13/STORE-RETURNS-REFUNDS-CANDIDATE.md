# SauceApproved Store — Return and Refund Policy (candidate)

**Status:** DRAFT — NOT APPROVED OR PUBLISHED  
**Prepared:** October 13, 2026  
**Store:** https://sauceapproved.com  
**Support:** smallzshon@gmail.com

## 1. Return request window

For eligible physical merchandise, contact us **within 30 days after receiving the item** to request a return. This matches the return window in the current Shopify policy. An approval and return instructions must be issued before mailing an item back.

## 2. Return condition

Items generally must be unworn or unused, in their received condition, with original tags and packaging where supplied, and accompanied by an order number, receipt, or other valid proof of purchase. We will consider circumstances and applicable law, including statutory protections for defective, damaged, misdescribed or incorrect goods.

## 3. Request a return

Email **smallzshon@gmail.com** with the order number, item, reason for return, and, for damage or incorrect items, a description and optional photographs. If a return is accepted, we will provide an authorized return address and directions, including any shipping-label arrangements. **Do not ship returns to a guessed address; the production return address is not yet verified for publication.**

## 4. Damaged, defective or incorrect goods

Inspect your order on arrival and contact us promptly about damage, defects, missing items or incorrect items. We will review and provide a legally appropriate repair, replacement, refund or other remedy. Nothing in this policy takes away mandatory consumer rights.

## 5. Exclusions and made-to-order items

Personalized or custom-made merchandise, gift cards, and certain clearance/sale goods may be excluded from **discretionary change-of-mind returns** where that restriction was disclosed before purchase and the law permits it. These exclusions do **not** eliminate legally required remedies for defective, damaged, incorrect or misdescribed goods. Product-specific exceptions must be verified against the actual fulfillment model before launch.

## 6. Return shipping charges

The current published storefront **Page** states a **$0 restocking fee**. For an approved change-of-mind return without a prepaid label, the customer arranges and pays the carrier's actual return postage; the store does not deduct separate undisclosed restocking or handling fees. Return instructions and the authorized return destination are provided after approval. Damaged, defective, misdescribed or incorrect items remain subject to the remedies required by law. **The official Shopify checkout Refund Policy object has not yet been synchronized because the connected Shopify apps lack `write_legal_policies` authorization.**

**Connecticut disclosure gate:** Connecticut General Statutes § 42-110aa requires a conspicuously displayed online return/exchange policy to specify the refund type (cash, payment credit, or store credit), time limits, fee amounts expressed in dollars or percent, and other conditions. The draft's uncertainty on fees is not acceptable for final publication. Confirm whether routine exchanges are offered or unavailable and specify that clearly. Source: https://prdext2.cga.ct.gov/2025/pub/chap_735c.htm

## 7. Refund method and timing

When a refund is approved after any required inspection, we will process it back to the original payment method unless the law or an agreed alternative requires otherwise. Our current storefront policy targets processing **within 10 business days** of approval; banks or payment processors may require additional posting time. Contact support if an approved refund does not appear after **15 business days**.

## 8. Exchanges

Contact support for options. A replacement may depend on stock and fulfillment arrangements. Where exchange processing is unavailable, we may ask you to complete the return process and place a separate order.

## 9. International customer rights

Customers in the European Union and other jurisdictions may have additional cooling-off or mandatory return rights that take precedence over narrower store policy. Any applicable withdrawal/return period and exceptions must be clearly disclosed before purchase. **A full cross-border compliance review remains required before offering additional markets.**

## 10. Digital goods and software access

This 30-day merchandise policy **does not apply** to software or digital access products. Hercules Titan Founding Access, Studio Founding Pilot, and monthly Studio subscriptions have separately disclosed product-specific terms. Candidate one-time Titan/Studio Founding Pilot refund terms currently contemplate a **7-calendar-day** request window; those terms require separate owner approval and cannot silently supersede this physical-goods policy.
