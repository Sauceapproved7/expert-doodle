## 1. Scope

This notice describes personal-information practices when you visit SauceApproved's Shopify-powered store, create a customer account, browse products, place an order, request a return, communicate with us, or interact with marketing features. Third-party websites may have their own policies.

## 2. Personal information

Depending on how you interact with the store, the information processed may include:
- identity and contact details, including name, email, phone and delivery/billing address;
- account settings, identity/session records and preferences;
- order, cart, transaction, fulfillment, refund and support records;
- payment confirmations or payment-related identifiers from authorized processors (payment-card credentials are ordinarily handled by processors);
- device, browser, IP address, cookies, website use and fraud-prevention telemetry;
- marketing choices, communication preferences and advertising interaction data where corresponding features are enabled.

Information may come from you, devices and cookies, Shopify, service providers, and legally permitted integrations or advertising partners.

## 3. Uses

We process information to operate the store; fulfill and deliver orders; manage payments, returns and customer service; provide account functions; prevent fraud and protect systems; maintain business records; comply with law; and conduct marketing or analytics where permitted and with required choices or consent.

## 4. Shopify and service providers

Shopify hosts the store and processes information to provide and improve its platform. We may disclose only relevant information to authorized payment processors, delivery and fulfillment companies, customer-support tools, analytics providers and advertising services, depending on the integrations enabled. The final published notice must list or link to the material providers/data-sharing categories supported by production evidence; an integration under evaluation is not necessarily active.

Shopify may separately process certain data under its own policy. Learn more at https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy/app-users and https://privacy.shopify.com/en.

## 5. Marketing, targeted advertising and cookies

Cookies or similar technologies may support shopping carts, accounts, fraud detection, analytics and optional advertising. When applicable law requires consent or an opt-out, we will honor those choices. Promotional emails include an unsubscribe method. Transactional emails, such as order receipts, may still be sent.

Where the law requires a choice to opt out of data sharing for targeted advertising, customers may use the available store privacy controls or contact smallzshon@gmail.com. **Confirm the actual opt-out-page URL, consent configuration and Global Privacy Control behavior before publication; do not claim an unverified mechanism works.**

## 6. Disclosure and protection

We share data as necessary for transactions and legitimate operations; at your direction; or when reasonably necessary to respond to lawful requests, enforce rights, handle legal proceedings, or protect users. We do not claim that every service provider has identical data practices or security safeguards.

## 7. Retention and security

Data is retained as necessary to fulfill orders, support accounts, administer returns, satisfy tax/accounting and other legal duties, handle disputes, and prevent fraud. Retention varies by data type and provider. We use appropriate security controls, but no system provides absolute security. **Do not insert a universal deletion period without system evidence.**

## 8. Rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, opt-out of certain disclosures or targeted advertising, withdrawal of consent, or appeal of a decision. We may verify a requester's identity and apply lawful exceptions. Submit requests to smallzshon@gmail.com. You can also use Shopify's privacy portal where applicable: https://privacy.shopify.com/en.

## 9. Children's privacy

This store is not directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children contrary to applicable law. Parents or guardians may contact us about information submitted by a child.

## 10. International processing

Our service providers may process information in other states or countries. Where applicable, legally required transfer protections and notices apply. **Do not claim any particular transfer mechanism without validating actual providers and customer markets.**

## 11. Changes and contact

We may update this notice with a revised effective date and any notice legally required. Email privacy inquiries to smallzshon@gmail.com. Additional identity and address disclosures should be checked against the operating entity and applicable state or regional requirements.
