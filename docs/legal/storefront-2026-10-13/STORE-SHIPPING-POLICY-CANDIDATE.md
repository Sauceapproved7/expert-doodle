## 1. Shipping availability

SauceApproved ships eligible physical products to destinations supported by the shipping services available at checkout. Available destinations and services can change. Orders cannot be accepted for delivery destinations the store or fulfillment provider does not support.

## 2. Processing and fulfillment

After an order is accepted and payment verified, items enter the applicable fulfillment workflow. Processing may vary by product availability, customization, print-on-demand production, weekends and holidays. **No fixed processing-time guarantee is stated until the actual provider/service-level evidence is confirmed.**

## 3. Shipment deadlines and legally required delay options

We will have a reasonable basis for any shipping timeframe presented when an order is placed. If no specific timeframe is communicated, the FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule generally requires a reasonable basis to expect shipment within 30 days. If we cannot ship within the promised or legally applicable timeframe, we will provide the legally required notice and a choice to agree to a delayed shipment or cancel for a prompt refund. We will not treat shipment estimates as a waiver of those rights.

**Operations gate:** Before publication or acceptance of orders, verify Printify or other fulfillment capability and implement order-level deadline tracking, customer delay notices, documented consent and prompt refunds. A print-on-demand integration alone does not prove this is operational.

**Legal source:** https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule

## 5. Shipping charges and estimates

Available delivery methods, charges, and any estimated delivery windows are shown during checkout when supported. Shipping prices and eligibility may depend on destination, weight, item type and selected service. Quoted delivery windows are estimates unless the checkout specifically guarantees one.

## 4. Tracking and delivery notifications

Where the carrier supplies tracking, we will provide available tracking information through the order or shipping notification. Shipping updates may be delayed between label creation, carrier pickup and carrier scans. A shipping-label creation event alone does not prove the package was delivered.

## 6. Delays, missing parcels and wrong addresses

For significant delays or missing deliveries, contact **smallzshon@gmail.com** with the order number. We will investigate with the carrier or fulfillment provider and follow applicable consumer law. Please verify your delivery address when placing the order; contact us quickly if it requires correction. Address changes cannot always be made after fulfillment begins.

## 7. Damaged deliveries

If an item arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, notify us promptly with your order information and a description (and photographs if helpful). We will address the issue under the Return and Refund Policy and applicable law.

## 8. International shipping and import charges

International shipping is offered only where enabled at checkout. Any customs fees, duties, import taxes or restrictions must be disclosed in accordance with law and checkout rules. **Before publication, verify whether duties/taxes are collected at checkout or charged on delivery for each served market; no unverified allocation of charges is promised here.**

## 9. Digital purchases

Software subscriptions, downloadable goods and Hercules Titan access are not shipped as physical merchandise. Their access and delivery are governed by the relevant software product terms and entitlement process.

## 10. Contact

For questions about shipping, delays or delivery, email **smallzshon@gmail.com**.
