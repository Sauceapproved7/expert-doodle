## Channel policy
- Website/SEO: unique product facts, correct canonical URL, clean collection/product title, valid availability, size chart, accessible images, original copy, schema if supported, shipping/returns/terms.
- Google Merchant: pursue free listings only after product policies, feed accuracy and shipping/return settings are verified; free placements are not guaranteed.
- TikTok, Instagram, Shorts: human-accurate product demos and source-locked scripts. Approved JPEG/WebP where publishing provider requires them; prevent duplicate retry. Do not post identical creative repeatedly or exceed plan quota.
- Pinterest: original product visuals, destination-specific UTM tags and product disclosure.
- YouTube: demonstrated Hercules functionality and limitations. No simulated buyer success presented as real.
- Email: opt-in welcome/abandoned checkout only where consent and jurisdictional requirements are met. Include identity, applicable physical mailing address, unsubscribe mechanism and required ad disclosures.
- B2B outreach: personally relevant business prospects, verified contact basis and reputation controls. Do not harvest private data or send bulk unwanted contacts.
- Creators/affiliate: real commissions only on qualifying tracked orders; full relationship disclosures. No purchases of fake followers or reviews.
- Paid ads: remain OFF until checkout works, all claims have real evidence, target and event integrity validated, and authorized budget caps exist.

## Red/blue doctrine
RED examines promises, funnel obstacles, price surprises, consent gaps, provider rule violations, source truth, tracking loss, and competitor confusion.
BLUE prevents publishing under false proofs, missing customer basis, invalid destinations, attribution fabrication, deceptive scarcity, inappropriate institutional references, brand impersonation, leaked customer data and runaway spend.
PURPLE closes recorded weaknesses with tests and monitored, bounded corrective controls.

Avoid black-hat operations: fake reviews, bots, prohibited scraping, impersonation, deceptive urgency, undisclosed sponsorship, unauthorized personal-data sales, click manipulation or hidden checkout fees. These are threat patterns to recognize and defend against, not implementation requirements.

## Weekly operating system

Wednesday: Review and prepare eligible channel distributions; only release through separately authorized compliant publishing controls.
Thursday: Follow up only with properly permissioned leads and improve actual product messaging.

## Measurement contracts
Every campaign must have a stable ID, offer ID, canonical destination, utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, launch release status, claim-evidence refs and stop condition.
Tracked events: site session (with bot/owner ambiguity stated), qualified product engagement, add-to-cart, checkout start, paid order (verified provider), fulfillment/delivery, refund, complaint, repeat customer. Sensitive personal data never enters public attribution artifacts.
On no verified sales or unreliable attribution, mark CPA, ROAS, LTV and conversion lift **unverified**, never inferred.
