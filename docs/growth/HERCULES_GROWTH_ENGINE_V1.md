# Hercules Growth Engine v1

Owned operating specification for SauceApproved Enterprise LLC.

## Objective
Create a repeatable, organic-first acquisition loop for SauceApproved and Hercules without depending on paid advertising.

## Core loop
1. DISCOVER — find explicit demand, recurring pain, and high-intent conversations.
2. QUALIFY — map each signal to product, ICP, urgency, and permissible contact path.
3. CREATE — produce one useful asset: answer, demo, landing page, comparison, case study, or free tool.
4. DISTRIBUTE — publish only to channels whose rules permit it; no spam or deceptive engagement.
5. CAPTURE — route interested users to a clear SauceApproved/Hercules destination with attributable source tags.
6. FOLLOW UP — use consent-aware lifecycle messaging and bounded outbound.
7. CONVERT — measure activation, qualified lead, checkout start, paid conversion, and retention.
8. LEARN — feed outcomes back into positioning, product, and channel priorities.

## Organic channel stack
- Founder-led/build-in-public content
- Social listening and intent discovery
- Search: SEO plus AEO/GEO
- Launch platforms and relevant software directories
- Community participation, including rule-compliant Reddit participation
- Cold outreach to qualified prospects
- Email onboarding and lifecycle
- Referral/affiliate loops
- Engineering-as-marketing: small useful free tools

## Hercules modules
### Signal Scout
Collects public, permitted demand signals. Outputs source, problem, audience, intent, confidence, and recommended next action.

### ICP Router
Maps a signal to a SauceApproved offer. Rejects weak-fit or ambiguous leads instead of forcing outreach.

### Asset Forge
Turns validated pain into channel-specific assets. Every asset has one audience, one problem, one promise, one proof point, and one CTA.

### Distribution Router
Maintains channel rules, required formats, source URLs, campaign tags, and publish approval state. Default is DRAFT; publishing requires an authorized path.

### Lead Ledger
Tracks source -> visit -> lead -> activation -> checkout -> customer. No invented attribution.

### Experiment Engine
Runs one-variable experiments with a hypothesis, primary metric, guardrail metric, start/stop rule, and evidence log.

## Engineering-as-marketing rule
Prefer free tools that solve a narrow problem the paid product solves more deeply. A candidate must have:
- a specific search/community demand signal;
- useful standalone output;
- a natural bridge to Hercules;
- low operating cost;
- no requirement for sensitive credentials.

Initial candidates:
1. Shopify Launch Readiness Checker
2. Small-Business Website Conversion Grader
3. AI Workflow ROI Estimator

## Weekly operating cadence
MON: ingest demand signals; rank opportunities.
TUE: build one high-value asset/tool improvement.
WED: distribute to eligible channels and perform qualified outreach.
THU: follow up on engaged leads; improve landing/onboarding friction.
FRI: reconcile funnel metrics and source attribution.
WEEKEND: repurpose proven material; do not amplify losing experiments.

## Metrics
North-star: activated qualified users.
Leading: qualified signals, meaningful replies, landing conversion, tool completions, email opt-ins.
Revenue: checkout starts, paid conversions, revenue by attributable source.
Quality: activation rate, retention, refunds, unsubscribes, spam/abuse complaints.

## Guardrails
- Organic-first; paid acquisition is not required.
- Respect platform/community rules and consent requirements.
- No purchased engagement, fake reviews, impersonation, scraping behind authentication, or spam.
- No publishing or sending solely because content was generated.
- No financial/commerce unlock is implied by marketing readiness.
- Secrets and customer data never enter campaign artifacts.
- All attribution claims require recorded evidence.

## First 30-day target
Prove one repeatable acquisition loop:
public demand signal -> useful asset/free tool -> SauceApproved destination -> qualified lead -> activation -> measured outcome.

Scale only after the loop has attributable evidence.
