# SauceApproved Studio — Shopify Hero Art Direction v1

Status: Approved creative direction
Date: 2026-09-30

## Purpose

Primary front-facing Shopify hero artwork for SauceApproved Studio — Founding Pilot Access.

## Composition

- Square ecommerce hero composition.
- Centerpiece: a substantial old-school cinema camera, not a modern phone/camcorder.
- Camera body: dark metal/chrome, tactile mechanical controls, premium studio-build feel.
- Hercules layer: subtle modern intelligence integrated into the camera rather than pasted on top—precision interface details, refined lens glow/reflection, controlled technical accents.
- SauceApproved identity: original SauceApproved mark integrated naturally into the camera body.
- Background: dark professional film studio, controlled cinematic lighting, strong depth, uncluttered negative space.
- No random people, stock characters, fake creators, or unrelated props.
- No borrowed third-party logos or protected camera-brand marks.

## Typography

Primary text: SauceApproved Studio
Secondary text: Powered by Hercules

Keep typography minimal. The camera remains the dominant visual.

## Brand intent

Classic filmmaking equipment rebuilt for an AI-native SauceApproved production studio: vintage filmmaking DNA, modern Hercules execution.

The visual must feel premium, original, durable, cinematic, and commercially credible—not like generic AI art.

## Shopify presentation rules

- This is the lead/front-facing Studio image.
- Do not use the SoundWorld gift as the dominant visual.
- Do not imply hardware is included with Studio itself; the SoundWorld launch gift remains a separate promotional entitlement governed by its launch terms.
- Keep the image readable at Shopify mobile-card size.
- Preferred source master: 2048 × 2048 or larger square.
- Shopify delivery target: high-quality square image, optimized after source render.
- Alt text: "SauceApproved Studio vintage cinema camera powered by Hercules"

## Generation prompt

Create a premium square ecommerce hero image for “SauceApproved Studio.” Center a substantial vintage professional cinema camera with a dark metal and chrome body, tactile mechanical controls, and a large cinematic glass lens. Reimagine the camera as an AI-native production instrument powered by Hercules: subtle precision interface details and a restrained futuristic lens reflection integrated into the hardware, not floating sci-fi clutter. Integrate the original SauceApproved identity naturally into the camera body. Place it in a dark professional film-studio environment with controlled cinematic lighting, depth, and clean negative space. No people, no stock characters, no third-party camera logos, no busy background. Minimal typography only: “SauceApproved Studio” and smaller “Powered by Hercules.” The result should feel like classic filmmaking equipment rebuilt for a modern proprietary AI studio—premium, strong, original, cinematic, realistic, and commercially polished.

## Negative direction

Avoid: plastic toy look, generic cyberpunk neon, excessive holograms, clutter, fake brand marks, random people, cheap stock-photo lighting, cartoon camera proportions, illegible text, unrelated futuristic city backgrounds, or changing the SauceApproved identity.
